# BLUF — needle found

**Answer:** No. As of 2026-06-29 the U.S. has **not developed or adopted** a central bank digital currency (CBDC);
federal policy has moved to **prohibit** one. A 2025-01-23 executive order bars agencies from establishing or issuing
a CBDC, and the Federal Reserve states it will not issue one without an authorizing law. Statutory bans are advancing
in Congress, but a permanent standalone statute is **not confirmed enacted**. `[retrieved]`
