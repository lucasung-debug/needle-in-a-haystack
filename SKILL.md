# research - Research Governance Gate

Obey `research.md` before producing, merging, or validating any research conclusion.

1. Load `research.md`.
2. Apply LAW 0, the 6 axes, BYOK/free-first rules, and the 4 layers.
3. Require the mandatory compliance block in every research output.
4. Mark outputs that skip provenance or verification as INVALID and redo them.

This skill is a gate, not a research collector. It governs other research skills and router/query-planner merges.
